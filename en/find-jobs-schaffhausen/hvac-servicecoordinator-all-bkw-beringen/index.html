Full description

We make habitats worth living.

You are an organizational talent, think solution-oriented and enjoy connecting customers, technology and people? Then we will look for you! Lutz Dietrich AG is a versatile company in the areas of heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold and sanitation.

Your tasks

  • Service coordination: receiving, processing and coordinating service orders and fault reports.
  • Operational planning: Efficient planning and scheduling of our service installers taking into account urgency, availability and cost-effectiveness.
  • Customer support: Competent telephone and written support of our customers as well as agreement and coordination of service appointments.
  • Order processing: Administrative support of service orders from entry to invoicing.
  • Material coordination: organization and ordering of required spare parts and materials in cooperation with our specialist departments.
  • Interface Management: Close cooperation with service assemblers, project managers, suppliers and internal departments.
  • Quality assurance: monitoring open orders, monitoring feedback and ensuring timely processing.
  • Process optimization: Active participation and further development of our service processes.

You bring that with you

  • Completed basic technical or commercial training, ideally with experience in building technology (HLKS).
  • Experience in service coordination, scheduling or technical administration is advantageous.
  • Technical understanding and enjoyment of organizational tasks.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method.
  • Strong communication skills and enjoyment of direct customer contact.
  • Resilience and the ability to keep track even in hectic situations.
  • Secure handling of MS Office and ideally experience with Abacus or comparable ERP systems.
  • Teamwork, initiative and a high sense of responsibility.

More important than a perfect CV are your personality, your commitment and your willingness to take responsibility. We do not want requests from personnel service providers.
