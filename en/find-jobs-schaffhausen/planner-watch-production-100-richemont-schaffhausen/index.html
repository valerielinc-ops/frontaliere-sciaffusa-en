Planner Watch Production (100%)

Duration: Permanent Start: January 2027

As a Planner Watch Production team, you are responsible for the smooth planning and control of our watch production – from production order to on-time delivery.

Your mission to the IWC.

  • Self-responsible order issuance and fine planning in watch assembly as well as preparation of the monthly production plan
  • Ensuring component availability – from proactive deadline monitoring to systematic fault analysis to escalation – in close cooperation with production, procurement and sales
  • Creation and coordination of engraving and special orders
  • Creation and further development of IST analyses, evaluations and ad hoc reports and tools
  • Co-design and optimization of planning and business processes, as well as participation in cross-divisional projects

How you become part of the journey.

Training and experience

  • Basic technical or commercial training with further training for technicians or comparable studies – ideally with a focus on supply chain, production planning
  • 1-2 years of professional experience in production planning, preferably in the industrial manufacturing environment
  • Good technical understanding in the production context

Technical competences

  • Good SAP knowledge in production planning
  • Experienced handling of MS Office, especially Excel
  • Strong analytical capabilities and high affinity for data and systems
  • Flowing knowledge of German and English in words and writing

Personal competences

  • Structured, independent working method with the willingness to think outside the box
  • Rapid comprehension and the ability to recognize and link complex relationships
  • Results-oriented and team-oriented action
  • Strength of communication and enjoyment of cross-cutting cooperation
