Full description

Every year, the medical clinic serves 3,500 inpatient and 6,000 outpatient patients. The offer includes a broad general internal medicine as well as an extended basic care in the field of angiology, endocrinology/diabetology, gastroenterology, nephrology, cardiology, neurology, oncology and pneumology/sleep medicine. The Department of Pneumology offers a wide range of pneumological examination and treatment methods for inpatient and outpatient patients, including sleep medicine. We are a category B FMH Clinic for Pneumology.

Tasks

  • Versatile and responsible work in the ambulatory and stationary setting
  • Independent management of the pneumological consultation incl. Sleep medicine
  • Performation of bronchoscopies incl. EBUS, Pleural Interventions and Consiliar Care of Inpatients
  • Technical supervision and participation in the education, training and further training of assistant and senior physicians
  • Close interdisciplinary cooperation within and outside the Cantonal Hospital
  • Participation in internal medicine operations
  • A dedicated, familiar and well-established team

Requirements

  • Specialist title Pneumology FMH and General Internal Medicine FMH (or corresponding MEBEKO equivalents)
  • In-depth clinical experience across the spectrum of pulmonology
  • High social skills, communication skills and enjoyment of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods
  • Commitment and initiative as well as interest in further developing pneumology & sleep medicine together with the existing team

We offer

Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well.

The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
