Full description

The hospitals Schaffhausen are in the midst of the digital transformation. Modern technologies such as cloud services, AI and networked medical technology open up new possibilities – but also place high demands on IT security.

As an ICT Security Engineer, you take on a central role in protecting our critical infrastructure and make a direct contribution to safe hospital operations. Tasks: • Responsibility for the operation and further development of our ICT security solutions • Monitoring security systems, early detection of threats and initiating targeted measures • Analysis and handling of security incidents, including ensuring sustainable resolution • Identification of vulnerabilities, risk assessment and coordination of appropriate countermeasures • In your role, you advise ICT projects and accompany new technologies from a security point of view • Active further development of our security processes and architecture • Support for risk analysis and the expansion and operation of the ISMS • Close cooperation with IT, departments and external partners • Promoting security awareness throughout the organisation Requirements • Completed training or studies in computer science or IT security or equivalent • Several years of experience in a similar function • In-depth knowledge of network and system security, firewalls, endpoint security, vulnerability management and identity & access management • Experience with cloud and hybrid IT environments • Knowledge of information security management, risk analysis and standards such as ISO 27001 or NIST is beneficial • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method • Ability to understandably assess risks and communicate them appropriately • Independent, responsible personality with team spirit We offer: Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well. The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
