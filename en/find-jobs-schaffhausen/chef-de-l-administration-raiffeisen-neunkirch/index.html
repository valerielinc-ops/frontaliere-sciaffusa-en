09.09.2026 Neunkirch

Become part of our team!

For Raiffeisenbank Schaffhausen, a modern, successful and locally oriented bank with headquarters in Schaffhausen and a branch office in Neunkirch, we are looking for a committed, management-strong and sales-oriented personality as head of branch office 80-100% as a result of retirement of the previous branch manager for our branch office in Neunkirch as of 01.03.2027 or by agreement. In this role, you lead and develop a dedicated team of four employees and actively shape the further development of the office.

What awaits you?

  • You lead the Neunkirch office professionally and disciplinary, develop your employees further and promote a performance-oriented and appreciative leadership culture.
  • You are responsible for the sales, revenue, cost and quality goals of your office and ensure efficient use of human and financial resources.
  • You manage your own customer portfolio in the investment and asset customer segment and accompany your customers in the areas of investing, provisioning, financing, hedging and living.
  • You recognize customer potential, use sales opportunities in a targeted manner, gain new customers and position Raiffeisen as a holistic solution provider.
  • You actively promote the internal and external brokerage business and work closely with internal specialists and external partners.
  • You support your team in challenging consulting and investment topics, ensure a high quality of advice and act as a professional contact person within the bank.
  • You represent Raiffeisenbank Schaffhausen in Neunkirch and the region, actively maintain your network and engage in customer events, market development measures and campaigns.
  • You ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and internal guidelines.

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training and higher bank-specific training (HF, FH, bank economist, financial planner or similar).
  • Several years of experience in investment and asset consulting as well as in the support of demanding customers.
  • Initial management experience or proven experience in the professional management of employees.
  • In-depth knowledge in the areas of investing, provisioning and financing.
  • Strong advisory, sales and acquisition strength.
  • Entrepreneurial thinking as well as high results and customer orientation.
  • Authentic personality with high social competence, assertiveness and pleasure in the development of employees.
  • Independent, structured and responsible working methods.
  • Secure handling of digital consulting and sales solutions.
  • Certification or the willingness to acquire it.

Are you an entrepreneurial leader with a passion for customer consulting? Do you want to look after your own customer portfolio, successfully develop your team and actively contribute to the sustainable success of our bank? Then we should definitely get to know each other.
