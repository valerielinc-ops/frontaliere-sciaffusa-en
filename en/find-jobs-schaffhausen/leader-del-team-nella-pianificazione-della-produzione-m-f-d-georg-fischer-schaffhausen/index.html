Full description

Due to a natural departure, we are looking for a new head for our three-member production planning team at our site in Schaffhausen. In this function, you ensure the entire production planning and control for the Schaffhausen and Subingen plant while maintaining optimal deadlines and capacity utilization. This varied position is particularly suitable for people who like to work operationally as well as strategically and enjoy the development and motivation of a team of specialists.

Their main tasks

  • Strategic & Operational Planning: Responsibility for the entire production planning and control to ensure optimum timeliness and capacity utilization
  • Material Management: Ensuring sound inventory and material management to avoid bottlenecks while optimizing stockpiles
  • Internal logistics: Close coordination with internal logistics to ensure smooth material flows from acceptance of goods to delivery on the lines
  • Process optimization: Continuous development of planning and logistics processes in the sense of lean management
  • Team management: disciplinary and professional management as well as further development of the team in production planning

Your profile

  • Successfully completed studies in supply chain management, logistics, industrial engineering or a comparable qualification
  • At least 5 years of experience in production planning of an industrial company
  • In-depth, in-depth knowledge in materials management, as well as proven knowledge of internal logistics processes
  • Good knowledge of SAP and other relevant tools
  • Experience in HR management and development
  • Conviction and ability to communicate in a winning way beyond departments and hierarchical levels
