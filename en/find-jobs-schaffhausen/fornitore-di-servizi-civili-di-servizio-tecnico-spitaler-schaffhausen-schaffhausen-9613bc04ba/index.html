Full description

Obligation specification 113539. The minimum period is 1 month. No night or weekend services are planned. We ask you to name your desired period for civil service in the application letter. Tasks:

  • Technical assistance (e.g. assistance in the repair and maintenance of buildings and technical facilities throughout the hospital area)
  • Environmental work (e.g. assistance in basic and maintenance work in public areas, wiping, leaching, snow clearing, emptying of waste buckets, mowing lawns, cutting dams)
  • Disposal (e.g. assistance in gathering waste, sorting according to different categories, professional disposal)
  • Transport service (e.g. assistance with the internal transport service, messengers, food distribution, mail distribution, collection of dirt washing) Requirements:
  • Professional training
  • punctuality
  • Reliability
  • independent and flexible work
  • Motivation We offer: You want to get family and profession well? With our in-house crèche right on the area of the cantonal hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works but does well. The Spitäler Schaffhausen include the Cantonal Hospital, the Psychiatric Services, the Praxen Radiology and Cardiology Posthof Neuhausen and the Practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With over 1'700 employees, we secure the extended medical and nursing basic care of the Schaffhausen region. Every year we care about 11'000 stationary and 90'000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
