Full description

We make habitats worth living.

Are innovation, sustainability and design as important to you as they are to us? Then you are at the right address with us. Become part of our dynamic and dedicated team.

Your tasks

  • You lead the ventilation department professionally, personnel and economically, develop your team further and promote a binding, solution-oriented cooperation.
  • You are responsible for the budget, sales and results of your department, check the relevant key figures and derive measures to ensure profitability.
  • You maintain existing customer relationships, win new orders and work together with your team to develop needs-based solutions and offers.
  • You control the order processing from the offer to the planning and assembly up to commissioning and handover, support with technical and commercial questions and ensure quality, deadlines and costs.
  • You plan human and material resources ahead of time, coordinate the cooperation between project management, assembly and service as well as with other trades and optimize the processes of your department.
  • You ensure compliance with safety, hygiene and quality requirements as well as fire and noise protection requirements.

Your expertise

  • You have completed training in ventilation or building technology as well as a further technical or business qualification, for example at the level of higher specialist examination, HF or FH.
  • You bring several years of experience in ventilation and air conditioning technology as well as in the management of projects and teams and know the requirements for economic order processing.
  • You think entrepreneurially, act customer- and result-oriented and combine negotiating skills with a structured way of working. With clear communication and joy in leadership, you give your team orientation.
  • You will act confidently and bindingly towards customers, suppliers and partners, have a good knowledge of German in both words and writing and have a category B driving licence.
