Full description

Do you want to use analytical know-how to get to the bottom of complex technical issues and make a direct contribution to product quality and the development of new solutions?

Our microscopy and analysis laboratory is an important global competence within the Georg Fischer Group. Using modern optical and spectroscopic analysis methods, we investigate and evaluate materials and plastic components. Our expertise is particularly in demand in the clarification of technical damage and cause cases and at the same time provides valuable insights for the further development of existing and the development of new products and solutions.

As a Microscopy & Analytics Expert, you combine scientific-analytical work with concrete questions from industrial practice. They not only analyze, but also interpret results, recognize connections and derive sound insights from them.

You work closely with production, research & development and other specialist areas, both locally and internationally. A versatile specialist position for people who like to get to the bottom of things, understand complex relationships and derive concrete solutions from analysis results.

Their main tasks

  • Analysis and assessment of materials and plastic components using optical examination methods
  • Performing IR measurements as well as professional interpretation and evaluation of spectra
  • Evaluation, documentation and comprehensible preparation of test results in technical reports
  • Development and maintenance of basic and reference data for plastics
  • Evaluation, development and introduction of new analysis and testing methods
  • Active participation in interdisciplinary projects with production, R&D and other specialist areas
  • Establishment and maintenance of a network of external testing laboratories as well as coordination and commissioning of external investigations
  • Technical support and representation in adjacent laboratory areas, in particular in the chemical and materials testing laboratory

Your profile

  • Higher degree in materials science, plastics technology, chemistry or a similar discipline or qualified training as a chemistry or Physics laboratory
  • At least two years of professional experience in an analytical laboratory or industrial environment
  • Enthusiasm for practical laboratory work and pleasure in systematically getting to the bottom of complex technical issues
  • Structured, careful and solution-oriented working method with a good eye for the essentials
  • Strong analytical skills as well as the ability to identify connections and derive sound conclusions
  • Ability to understand and communicate complex technical issues to the point
  • Enjoy working with different disciplines and in an international environment
  • Good knowledge of German and English
