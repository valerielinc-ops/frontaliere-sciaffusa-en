Full description

In our interdisciplinary emergency center at the Cantonal Hospital of Schaffhausen we offer a comprehensive and high-quality medical care, with over 30,000 people per year – adults and children – being treated and cared for comprehensively. We offer an exciting challenge in a family team with great development potential. Tasks:

  • You take the triage (adults and children) calmly, thought and with a look that looks more than just symptoms
  • You decide what is urgent and who you make the waiting time more bearable with patience and compassion
  • You can find a family, value-adding atmosphere with us and take on a lot of responsibility Requirements:
  • Training as a medical/practice worker or equivalent medical training at level EFZ, HF, FH or NDS
  • At least 4 years of professional experience in an acute hospital or a medical practice
  • A large portion of service orientation and empathy, even if it becomes loud, hectic or emotional
  • Responsibility
  • humor, team spirit and desire to develop you professionally and humanly We offer: You want to get family and profession well? With our in-house crèche right on the area of the cantonal hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works but does well. The Spitäler Schaffhausen include the Cantonal Hospital, the Psychiatric Services, the Praxen Radiology and Cardiology Posthof Neuhausen and the Practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With over 1'700 employees, we secure the extended medical and nursing basic care of the Schaffhausen region. Every year we care about 11'000 stationary and 90'000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
