Full description

Our 8 bed intensive care unit with 3 telemetry places treats patients with acute cardiovascular disorders, life-threatening bronchopulmonary diseases, acute and chronic kidney failure, poisoning, life-threatening metabolic derailments and endogenous crises, disturbances of consciousness and comata as well as shocks of various causes. Patients with accident-related traumas and after extended operations are also treated postoperatively in our intensive care unit.

This is what awaits you

  • You take on the necessary cleaning and cleaning work and contribute to a smooth process in the intensive care unit.
  • You take on assignments in early and late service.
