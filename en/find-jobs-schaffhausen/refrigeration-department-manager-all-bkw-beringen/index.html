Full description

We make habitats worth living.

We offer you an exciting activity in refrigeration technology with a lot of variety and development potential. You can expect a versatile management task with creative leeway, a motivated team, attractive employment conditions and good training opportunities.

Your tasks

  • You lead the refrigeration department professionally, personnel and economically and develop your team further.
  • You are responsible for budget, sales and earnings, analyze key figures and derive measures to ensure profitability.
  • You maintain existing customer relationships, acquire new orders and work with your team to develop energy-efficient refrigeration solutions and suitable offers.
  • You control the entire order processing from the offer to the delivery of the equipment and ensure quality, deadlines and costs.
  • You plan resources, coordinate cooperation between project management, assembly and service, and ensure efficient processes.
  • You ensure compliance with safety, environmental and quality standards and develop the range of services with a focus on natural refrigerants and sustainable plant concepts.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed training as a refrigeration system installer EFZ, refrigeration system planner EFZ or a comparable basic technical training as well as a further technical or business qualification, for example at the level of higher technical examination, HF or FH.
  • You bring several years of experience in refrigeration technology as well as in the management of projects and teams. You know the requirements for an economic order processing and have in-depth knowledge in plant technology, in the handling of refrigerants and in the relevant safety and environmental requirements
  • Experience with natural refrigerants is beneficial.
  • You think entrepreneurially, act customer- and result-oriented and combine negotiating skills with a structured way of working. With clear communication and joy of leadership, you give your team orientation.
