Full description

With this position, we offer you a future-oriented training and career model that takes up the increasing connection between radiology and nuclear medicine. Our two areas work closely together and form an interdisciplinary team. The medical staff rotates between radiology and nuclear medicine and thus benefit from a versatile and interdisciplinary working environment.

This is what awaits you

  • Senior Medical Office (OA IV) with attractive employment conditions and long-term professional prospects
  • Complete specialist training in radiology in all relevant areas
  • Regular work in nuclear medicine with the opportunity to continue to contribute and expand your existing expertise
  • Broad nuclear medicine spectrum with SPECT/CT and access to PET in an outpatient centre in the Schaffhausen area
  • Individual opportunities for subspecialization and development of a personal focus
  • Personal and high-quality training with close professional support and a high level of personal responsibility
  • Perspective for further development into a senior position after successful completion of the double specialist training

This will take us further.

  • Specialist title in nuclear medicine or equivalent recognised qualification
  • Interested in a well-founded further education as a specialist for radiology
  • Delight in interdisciplinary collaboration and the combination of morphological and functional imaging
  • Commitment, initiative and interest in actively shaping the further development of our radiology and nuclear medicine
  • Team-oriented and responsible personality
  • Very good knowledge of German in language and writing

We would like to comprehensively accompany you on your way to becoming a double specialist in radiology and nuclear medicine and develop your individual training goals and long-term career prospects together with you. Precautionary benefit Your workplace is close to the city and yet in the middle of the green. During the break you breathe fresh forest air, do a jogging round or treat yourself to a coffee on the park bench. So the way to work feels like a walk and not like stress.
