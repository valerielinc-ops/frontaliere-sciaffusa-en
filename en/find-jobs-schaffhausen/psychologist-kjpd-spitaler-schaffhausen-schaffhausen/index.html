Full description

The hospitals Schaffhausen (SSH) provide basic child and youth psychiatric care in the canton Schaffhausen. The Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service (KJPD) provides outpatient and day-to-day clinical services for children, adolescents and their families on a public service mission.

This is what awaits you

  • You will conduct psychological assessments and diagnostic examinations in children and adolescents and work on a well-founded assessment of the individual situation.
  • You take on psychological interventions and support children, adolescents and their caregivers in dealing with psychological and psychosocial challenges.
  • You will deepen your diagnostic and therapeutic skills and receive professional support, supervision and support in your further development.
  • You work closely with the interdisciplinary team as well as with parents, schools, doctors and other specialists.
  • You are actively involved in the team, participate in case discussions and ensure careful documentation and reporting.

This will take us further.

  • You have a master’s degree in psychology or an equivalent recognised degree.
  • You enjoy working with children, adolescents and families and want to further develop your professional future in the child and adolescent psychiatric or psychotherapeutic field.
  • You meet young people and their caregivers empathetically, appreciatively and at eye level.
  • You enjoy working in an interdisciplinary manner, are open to different perspectives and actively participate in the collaboration.
  • You work reliably, independently and reflectively, are willing to learn and open to professional guidance and supervision.
  • You are ready to design the KJPD of the hospitals Schaffhausen together with our new head doctor KJPD.
