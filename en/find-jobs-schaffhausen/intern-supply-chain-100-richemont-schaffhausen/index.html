Intern Supply Chain Development (100%)

The primary focus of this internship is to accelerate our data & analytics transformation, enabling Supply Chain leadership to leverage data for enhanced decision-making and business development. You will play a key role in the creation and deployment of reports and dashboards using GCP products.

Start: April 2027

Duration: 6 months

YOUR MISSION AT IWC.

Support the transition from SAC to GCP

  • Build and implement Google Looker reports and dashboards aligned with business KPIs and management priorities
  • Translate business needs into reporting logic; explore and structure data sources to ensure data quality and availability for reliable reporting
  • Identify automation opportunities for daily use of data products
  • Share best practices and promote a data-driven culture
  • Improve report usability, storytelling, and layout

Assistance in strategic Supply Chain Development projects.

HOW YOU BECOME PART OF THE JOURNEY.

  • Academic background in Industrial Management, Data Science, Finance or similar
  • A strong interest in data, analytics, and how they can be applied to solve business problems
  • Knowledge of SQL and experience with data warehousing concepts and technologies are a plus
  • Hands-on personality with a strong business acumen and excellent communication and collaboration skills
  • First practical experience is required: Familiarity in project-based, analytical settings a plus, e.g., tech, consulting
  • Business fluent English skills are required, German is a plus
