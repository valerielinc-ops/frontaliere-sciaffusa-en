To support our logistics department, we are looking for a

Warehouse Specialist (100%)

Duration: Permanent Start: April 2027

Your mission to the IWC.

Acceptance of goods/transportation

  • Processing of incoming goods from orders
  • Independent management of precious metals (entry into, withdrawal from and storage)
  • Correspondence with purchasing, project managers and financial accounting
  • Bookings of the storage movement in SAP
  • Processing of production and service orders according to the 7-R rule
  • Independent management of the tool and auxiliary warehouse (booking in and out and storage)
  • Packaging, sending and recording of domestic and international deliveries
  • Loading and unloading of trucks
  • Sorting and distribution of postal items
  • Assistance with the annual inventory
  • Assistance in ensuring the availability of the required equipment
  • Miscellaneous administrative tasks

Cooperation

  • Directly under the management of the logistics department
  • Cooperation with purchasing, quality control, production, distribution, planning

How you become part of the journey.

  • Apprenticeship as logistician or equivalent
  • Experience in incoming goods and outgoing goods/forwarding
  • Experience with inventory
  • Material provision with small components is advantageous
  • SAP and MS Office
  • Forklift/PW driving licence
  • Very precise and precise work
  • Self-employed and committed work
  • Quality and customer-oriented
  • High sense of responsibility
  • Very good knowledge of German, English skills are advantageous
