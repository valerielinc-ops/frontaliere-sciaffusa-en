Full description

In this highly visible global role, you will be part of a team growing the industrial jointing machine portfolio through technical and application expertise, strategic portfolio management, and innovation, ensuring GF remains the preferred partner for complete piping system solutions in industrial applications such as microelectronics, the chemical process industry, water treatment, and other demanding industries.

This is an opportunity for an experienced, technically strong, commercially driven Product Manager who enjoys taking ownership, working across functions, and delivering global impact.

Global Product Manager – Industrial Jointing Technologies (m/f/d)

Your tasks

  • Define and execute the global product strategy and roadmap for industrial jointing machines, aligned with business objectives and the growth ambitions of the GF Strategy 2030
  • Own the complete product lifecycle, from market analysis, business cases, and product specifications through development, launch, pricing, portfolio optimization, and phase-out
  • Act as the technical and application expert for industrial jointing machines and related digital solutions, translating customer needs, operator requirements and market trends into competitive product offerings
  • Drive innovation and new product development initiatives in close collaboration with R&D, identifying growth opportunities through customer, competitor, and application insights
  • Lead global product launches and go-to-market activities, including sales enablement, training concepts, technical documentation, and commercial rollout.
  • Collaborate closely with R&D, Operations, Service, Marketing, and Sales Companies worldwide to ensure successful product development and market adoption
  • Represent GF at customer visits, OEM meetings, trade fairs, and industry events, strengthening market presence and capturing strategic market intelligence

Your profile

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, Electrical Engineering, Business Engineering, or a comparable technical discipline; additional business qualifications are a plus
  • Several years of Product Management experience in industrial capital equipment, machinery, automation solutions, or other technically complex B2B products with international market exposure
  • Strong technical understanding of machine technology, automation, and digital solutions
  • Experience with welding, joining, or processing equipment is highly desirable
  • Proven track record in product strategy, lifecycle management, product launches
  • Successful cross-functional stakeholder management and entrepreneurial mindset
  • Commercially minded with experience in business case development, pricing, market analysis, and value proposition management, as well as the ability to convince the sales organizations of the product
  • Fluent in English; additional languages such as German and/or Italian are an advantage
  • Willingness to travel internationally (approximately 25-35%)
