Full description

The field of radiology and nuclear medicine offers a wide range of investigation methods

  • Conventional radiology, digital fluoroscopy, computed tomography, magnetic resonance imaging, interventional radiology, mammography, ultrasound and nuclear medicine. In the Canton of Schaffhausen as well as in adjacent regions, we work closely with the assigning and post-treatment doctors and thus ensure comprehensive care. Our employees are continuously trained in parallel with technical development.

Tasks

  • You look after the patients during the examination and inform them about the course of the examination
  • You are responsible for scheduled examinations in the divided examination room
  • You coordinate and plan emergencies independently
  • You work in the performance and case management of radiology
  • You record the performance in the ultrasound
  • You work in the various fields of radiology

Requirements

  • You have successfully completed an education as an MPA
  • You have high social skills
  • You are used to an independent, responsible and quality-oriented way of working
  • You are a motivated and committed personality

We offer

Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well.

The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
