Full description

Every year we offer an internship in accounting for a student of the Handelsmittelschule. The internship starts at 12. July 2027 and lasts until 31. July 2028.

Which activities you learn in this internship and which application documents we need from you can be found here: Tasks: • Managing the Fund Psychiatry Center • Processing of clarifications and correspondence in the area of debtors and creditors • Creating Payment Agreements for Patients • Ensure receipt of invoices in the creditor workflow • Accounting for creditors • General administrative tasks Requirements • Letter of motivation • Tabular CV • School certificates Commercial School • If available: confirmations of previous internships We offer: Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well. The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
