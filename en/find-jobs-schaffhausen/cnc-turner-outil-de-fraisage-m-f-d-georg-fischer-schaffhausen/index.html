Full description

Schaffhausen is not only the headquarters of the Georg Fischer Group, but also a very important production site. From now on, we are looking for a CNC turner and milling cutter (m/f/d) in two-shift operation for one of our teams. Ideally, we are thus addressing a person with prior experience who appreciates to carry out this activity in a modern production environment and with a high degree of independence. The participation in the training of learners and the opportunity to actively participate in QA initiatives and process improvements round off this varied activity.

Their main tasks

  • Technical and on-time production of articles by CNC-assisted turning and milling
  • Programming, setting up and monitoring of CNC-supported turning and milling centers, as well as 5- and 3-axis milling machines (programming system Siemens NX, control system Fanuc)
  • Ensuring efficient machine utilization through the proper installation and safe transfer of the machines to the operator
  • Responsibility for timely, quality and productivity-oriented production.
  • Close cooperation with production planning and work preparation regarding timely and technical implementation
  • Continuous improvement of production processes, including assurance through documentation
  • Care of the learning polymechanics in machining

Your profile

  • Vocational training as a polymechanic, machinist, industrial mechanic or similar
  • Ideally 3-5 years of experience in handling CNC machines (turning and milling)
  • Experience in creating CNC programs (optimally Siemens NX)
  • Independent and proactive working methods
  • Motivation to not only adhere to processes and processes, but also to improve them
  • Flowing German skills are mandatory, English skills are advantageous

Dossiers of agencies are not considered, candidates are asked to apply directly, thank you!
