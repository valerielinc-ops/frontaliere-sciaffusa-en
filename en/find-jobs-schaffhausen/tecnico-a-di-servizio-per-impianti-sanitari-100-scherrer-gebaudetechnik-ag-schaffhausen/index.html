Full description

Do you know us? We are a Swiss company with expertise in sanitary, heating and ventilation technology as well as their complete planning. Our location and the bathroom exhibition are located directly on the Rhine, with around 60 employees mainly working throughout the canton and beyond. We belong to the Burkhalter Group, the leading provider of cross-trade building technology in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein.

Do you like customer contact and ensure that the water flows in the right direction? Then you are exactly right with us!

Their tasks

  • Independent handling of service/maintenance and repair work on sanitary facilities, troubleshooting and minor conversions in the residential and industrial sector
  • Understand and implement relationships between different departments for efficient problem solving in customer systems
  • On-site reception and customer advice of repair and service orders for preparation of the offer by our team in the office
  • Documentation of the work carried out using electronic tools
  • Assistance in measuring and obtaining materials
  • if necessary organization of appointment coordination with our customers
  • Active maintenance and further development of customer relationships
  • Participation in Picett Service at fixed intervals

Your profile

  • Completed training as a sanitary installer and/or heating installer with some years of professional experience in customer service
  • Strong understanding of technical relationships in building technology
  • Self-employed, exact and efficient working method with a high level of personal responsibility
  • Professional appearance and good manners towards customers, internal and external partners
  • Readiness to participate in the Pikett service at fixed intervals
  • Team-capable, reliable and service-oriented personality
  • Negotiable knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Valid ticket Cat. B

Our offer

  • Central workplace with good connection options
  • Varied and local work in a collegial team
  • You culture (open and respectful communication in a family mood)
  • Individual employee promotion and development through cooperative leadership style
  • Modern workplace and working instruments
  • Fixed assigned company car
  • 4.5 days week and at least. 5 weeks of holidays per year
  • 40 hours – week with flexible working hours
  • Good social benefits
  • Various employee events
