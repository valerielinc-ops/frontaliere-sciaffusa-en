Full description

The departments for nephrology and diabetology/endocrinology offer specialist consultation hours at the hospitals Schaffhausen. The outpatient assessment and treatment of patients is carried out on an assignment basis and in cooperation with the general practitioners of the region.

Tasks

  • Sending medical reports and correspondence
  • Organisation of patient consultation hours
  • Scheduling
  • General secretarial services for office hours and dialysis stations
  • Blood sampling and infusions
  • Performance of endocrinological functional tests
  • A well-founded introduction to versatile medical fields of work
  • A small and dedicated team

Requirements

  • Completed training as medical/practice assistant
  • Professional experience as an MPA or in acute-somatic care profession
  • Flexibility, organisational skills and responsibility
  • Stylistic German and good knowledge of medical terminology
  • Joy of teamwork and dealing with people
  • Technical flair and very good PC knowledge

We offer

Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well.

The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
