Full description

We are looking for an experienced Lab Automation Specialist to support qualification, validation, and lifecycle management activities for laboratory systems and equipment within a GMP-regulated pharmaceutical environment. In this role, you will collaborate closely with laboratory and quality teams to ensure compliance, operational excellence, and the reliable performance of critical laboratory systems.

Key Facts

  • Location: Schaffhausen, Switzerland
  • Work Model: Hybrid (3 days onsite per week)
  • Workload: 100%
  • Contract Duration: Until December 2027
  • Start Date: As soon as possible
  • Industry: Pharmaceutical
  • Language: English required, German is an advantage

Tasks

  • Plan and coordinate qualification activities for laboratory equipment in compliance with GMP and regulatory requirements
  • Configure and maintain Empower projects, instrument methods, processing methods, and system settings
  • Prepare and execute change controls related to equipment lifecycle management
  • Create qualification lifecycle documentation, including:
  • Impact Assessments
  • Risk Assessments
  • User Requirement Specifications (URS)
  • IQ/OQ/PQ protocols
  • Decommissioning documents
  • Develop and maintain SOPs and work instructions related to laboratory equipment lifecycle management
  • Identify and document applicable pharmacopeial requirements for analytical laboratory equipment
  • Provide operational support to QC and R&D laboratories
  • Troubleshoot laboratory systems and equipment issues
  • Coordinate with external vendors regarding technical support, maintenance, calibration, investigations, and qualification activities
  • Support continuous improvement initiatives within laboratory operations and compliance processes

Qualifications

Education

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Natural Sciences, Engineering, Chemistry, Biotechnology, Pharmaceutical Sciences, or a related field
  • Alternatively, equivalent technical training combined with relevant professional experience

Experience & Skills

  • Minimum 5 years of experience in a GMP-regulated pharmaceutical environment
  • Proven experience in qualification and validation of laboratory equipment and/or manufacturing systems
  • Hands-on experience with Empower and/or eLIMS in a regulated laboratory environment
  • Strong knowledge of equipment lifecycle management and qualification documentation
  • Experience preparing and managing IQ/OQ/PQ documentation, URS, risk assessments, and change controls
  • Familiarity with pharmacopeial requirements and laboratory compliance standards
  • Project management experience within regulated environments
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
  • Fluent English language skills

Preferred

  • Experience with SCADA and/or MES systems
  • Knowledge of QMS, ERP systems, document management systems, and change management tools
  • German language skills
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities
  • Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively within cross-functional teams
