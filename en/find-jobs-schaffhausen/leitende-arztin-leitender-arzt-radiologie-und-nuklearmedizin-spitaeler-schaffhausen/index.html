Full description

The Institute of Radiology and Nuclear Medicine at Spitäler Schaffhausen offers a wide range of research methods

  • Convenient radiology, digital illumination, computer tomography, magnetic resonance tomography, interventional radiology, mammography, ultrasound and nuclear medicine. We work in the canton of Schaffhausen as well as in adjacent regions closely with the doctors who are responsible for and after-treating, thus ensuring comprehensive care. The institute is equipped with digital direct radiography, four ultrasound devices, two CT and four MR devices (1.5 and 3 Tesla), a modern SPECT-CT and PET-CT, a fully digital illumination and angiography site, digital mammography and stereotaxy as well as osteodensitometry. In addition to imaging diagnostics with a focus on senology, oncology and diagnostic neuroradiology, numerous diagnostic and therapeutic image-controlled interventions are also carried out. Another focus is the MSK radiology. In addition, we have certification as a center for cardiac imaging. A RIS and PACS system with digital voice recognition is available at all workplaces. The Institute has a training authorization in radiology (category A) and diagnostic neuroradiology (one year). Tasks:
  • exciting and varied clinical-radiological and nuclear medicine
  • Cooperative atmosphere
  • Possibility to co-design and co-determination in a creative team
  • Possibility of research conducted by the chief physician if necessary
  • academic possibilities
  • attractive, innovative and safe hospital environment
  • Participation in services and cooperation in the whole institute Requirements:
  • Swiss doctoral diploma or equivalent recognition
  • Double medical title Radiology FMH and Nuclear Medicine or a European equivalent
  • dedicated and team-capable personality
  • social competence in dealing with patients and employees We offer: You want to get family and profession well? With our in-house crèche right on the area of the cantonal hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works but does well. The Spitäler Schaffhausen include the Cantonal Hospital, the Psychiatric Services, the Praxen Radiology and Cardiology Posthof Neuhausen and the Practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With over 1'700 employees, we secure the extended medical and nursing basic care of the Schaffhausen region. Every year we care about 11'000 stationary and 90'000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
