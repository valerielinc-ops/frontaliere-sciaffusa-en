Full description

Medical technology ensures that facilities and systems are maintained reliably at all our locations.

Tasks

  • On-site support for device malfunctions incl. Emergency measures
  • Repair and repair as well as coordination of the operations of external service providers
  • Implementation of safety and metrological controls and maintenance of medical devices and equipment
  • Documentation of the work carried out in accordance with legal requirements
  • Technical advice and instruction of users
  • Resulting tasks within medical technology

Requirements

  • Graduate degree as a medical technician HF, electrical technician or comparable training in the field of electronics or mechatronics
  • Professional experience in the hospital environment desirable in view of the legal requirements for the maintenance of medical devices
  • Responsibility, flexibility and resilience as well as open and respectful communication with all stakeholders
  • Commitment to team-oriented and interdisciplinary cooperation

We offer

Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well.

The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
