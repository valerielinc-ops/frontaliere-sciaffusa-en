Full description

Every day, the employees of the main kitchen, which is located in the Cantonal Hospital, cook around 1,600 meals for patients, employees, guests of hospitals Schaffhausen and other external customers. In order to cope with this, well-considered supply planning, carefully coordinated processes, smooth organisation and forward-looking food procurement are necessary.

This is what awaits you

  • Independent and professional management of employees and organization of the diet kitchen
  • Ensuring correct and patient-friendly food care in compliance with work processes and recipes
  • Active further development and optimization of processes and recipes (IDDSI stages)
  • Responsibility for quality and compliance with die specifications
  • Proactive cooperation with the management of the kitchen, nutritional therapy, speech therapy and the other kitchen areas
  • Responsibility for the professional development of employees
  • Administrative tasks in the SanaLogic catering program

This will take us further.

  • Completed training as diet cook / diet cook EFZ
  • Several years of professional experience in the diet kitchen, ideally in hospital or health care
  • Initial management experience and pleasure in accompanying, promoting and developing employees
  • Independent, structured and precise working method with a high sense of responsibility
  • Good IT knowledge and ideally experience with the SanaLogic catering software
  • Flexibility, resilience and willingness to engage with new situations and developments
  • Strong quality awareness and a high understanding of hygiene and patient safety
  • Team-oriented, open and solution-oriented personality with pleasure in interdisciplinary cooperation

Precautionary benefit Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well.
