Full description

A dedicated, competent and well-positioned team awaits you! The Department of Therapy is interdisciplinary in the fields of physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy and training therapy. We support patients throughout the entire treatment chain from IPS to outpatient follow-up – this broad spectrum allows you to contribute and develop your existing skills.

Tasks

  • Stationary deployment on geriatric rehabilitation and on the acute ward
  • Outpatient therapeutic activity in the own specialist focus
  • An interdisciplinary team with experienced physiotherapists and occupational therapists and speech therapists

Requirements

  • Diploma as physiotherapist at HF or FH level
  • Awareness of responsibility and resilience
  • Dynamic and initiative personality
  • Good German knowledge of language and writing

We offer: You want to put family and work well together? With our in-house nursery right on the grounds of the cantonal hospital, we support you in ensuring a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but does good.

The hospitals of Schaffhausen include the cantonal hospital, the psychiatric services, the practices of radiology and cardiology Posthof Neuhausen as well as the practice of neurology Schaffhausen Zentrum. With over 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care of the Schaffhausen region. Every year we provide care for approximately 11,000 inpatients and 90,000 outpatients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
