For our clinic for psychiatry and psychotherapy, we are looking for a senior physician/superior physician immediately or by appointment for the center of gravity retirement psychiatry.

The Breitenau Psychiatry Center is, in addition to the Child and Adolescent Psychiatric Service and the Cantonal Hospital, one of several locations of the Schaffhausen hospitals. The Breitenau Psychiatry Center in Schaffhausen’s Breite Quarter consists of the Clinic for Psychiatry and Psychotherapy with its inpatient, day-clinical and outpatient treatment services as well as long-term psychiatry. Tasks: • Management of an acute care unit with treatment focus "Age psychiatry" • Leading a team in performing inpatient and outpatient therapies • Case management of selected outpatients and inpatients • Further development of the competence focus "Age psychiatry" • Participation in the collegial background service of the clinic Requirements • Completed or advanced training as a specialist in psychiatry and psychotherapy • Advantageous is the existing focus title in old-age psychiatry and psychotherapy or experience in the psychiatric-psychotherapeutic treatment of elderly patients • Joy and talent to lead teams and implement optimal treatment strategies with patients We offer: Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well. The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
