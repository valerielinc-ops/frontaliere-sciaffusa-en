Full description

The Central Ambulatorium looks after patients in surgery and orthopaedics who come to us for preliminary examinations, follow-up checks or minor interventions. In addition, we take over the registration for the fields of urology, angiology, rheumatology and pneumology.

We attach great importance to teamwork – we support each other to ensure competent and heartfelt patient care. Appreciation and respectful cooperation are our top priority and shape our everyday work. We are proud of the positive atmosphere and the strong cooperation that distinguishes our team.
To strengthen our team, we are currently looking for two new team members. We are flexible with regard to the level of employment – please let us know in your application which amount of work you want.

Join us in the team and be part of it! Tasks: • Operation of patient switches and reception • Assistance at the office hours • Planning and organisation of specialist consultation hours • Set up telephone service and appointments • Maintaining electronic medical histories • Writing of outpatient investigation reports and correspondence • General secretarial work Requirements • Completed training as medical/practice assistant/EFZ • Professional experience of several years • You are a responsible, resilient personality with a clear service orientation • Organizational talent through and through – you always keep an overview and plan efficiently We offer: Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well. The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
