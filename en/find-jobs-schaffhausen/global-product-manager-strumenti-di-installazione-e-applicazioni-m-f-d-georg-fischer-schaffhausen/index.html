Role overview

In this highly visible global role, you will own and grow the global installation tooling portfolio through application expertise, strategic portfolio management, supplier collaboration and innovation, ensuring GF remains the preferred partner for complete piping system solutions. This is an exciting opportunity for a technically minded, application-focused and commercially driven product manager who enjoys taking ownership, identifying growth opportunities, and delivering impact on a global scale.

Global Product Manager - Installation Tools & Applications (m/f/d)

  • Define and execute the global product strategy and roadmap for the installation tooling portfolio, aligned with business objectives and growth targets
  • Operate as the business owner of the global tooling portfolio, independently identifying opportunities, challenging existing assumptions and driving strategic initiatives that support profitable growth
  • Identify growth opportunities through customer, market, competitor, and application analysis, translating insights into compelling product solutions
  • Drive innovation, product improvements, and cost optimization initiatives to enhance customer value, profitability, and market competitiveness
  • Act as the global application expert for installation tools and installation processes, understanding customer workflows, installer requirements and application challenges across infrastructure and industrial markets
  • Collaborate closely with R&D, engineering, marketing, operations, quality, and external suppliers to develop and deliver market-leading solutions
  • Monitor product performance, pricing, and profitability, while representing GF at customer visits, industry events, and strategic growth initiatives
