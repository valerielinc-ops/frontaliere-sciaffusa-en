Full description

More than 30,000 patients per year, adults and children, in an interdisciplinary emergency centre with real room for manoeuvre. In Schaffhausen, we are looking for a cadre doctor with a special function in emergency medicine with a workload of 80 – 100%. In a well-established team of a cantonal hospital, you can expect a professional, appreciative environment in which high medical quality, interdisciplinarity and interprofessionality shape the mission statement. This is what awaits you in this responsible task

  • You provide emergency medical patients with interdisciplinary care in a cadreic function
  • You assume leadership and further education responsibility for trainees of various levels and professions
  • You work interdisciplinary and interprofessionally with all departments of the hospital
  • You assume development tasks for the emergency center in the sense of the mission statement
  • In the future, you have the opportunity to take on relevant management tasks

This is what they bring with them.

  • Interdisciplinary focus Clinical Emergency Medicine (SGNOR) or shortly before its completion
  • Completed specialist training with recognition for Switzerland for foreign diplomas
  • Ideally, experience in senior medical function in interdisciplinary, clinical emergency medicine
  • Interested in taking on development and management tasks for the further development of the emergency center

That sets them apart.

  • Commitment and motivation for clinical work of high medical quality
  • Enjoy interdisciplinary and interprofessional cooperation in a team-oriented environment
  • Teaching and accompanying learners as a personal core concern

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Responsible medical practice in an interdisciplinary emergency center with over 30,000 patients annually
  • Employer share of 60 % of pension contributions for above-average pension provision
  • Flexible working models: part-time, job sharing also in management positions, extended maternity leave, unpaid leave or additional holidays instead of 13. Monthly wages
  • House-owned crèche directly on the hospital area
  • Furnished rooms if necessary for an uncomplicated start in the region
  • Employee parking spaces at attractive conditions, REKA checks with a discount of up to 20% and a wide range of internal training opportunities

Additionally for applicants from the EU

  • Support for MEBEKO recognition of your diploma
  • Assistance in organizing your journey to Switzerland
  • Assistance in finding accommodation through our internal rental department
