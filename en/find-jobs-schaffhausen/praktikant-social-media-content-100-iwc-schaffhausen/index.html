INTERN SOCIAL MEDIA & CONTENT (100%)

Duration: 6 months Start: November 2026

Join our team and become a key player in shaping our social media and content marketing campaigns. Reporting to the Team Lead, Social Media & Content, you will manage social media channels, develop engaging content, and collaborate with external and internal teams on innovative projects. This role also offers the opportunity to acquire an overview of the entire global brand marketing campaign scope, from orchestration to implementation. We are looking for someone with a passion for luxury, strong communication skills, and a proactive approach. If you are a detail-oriented team player with a keen eye for copy and design, we encourage you to apply!

YOUR MISSION AT IWC.

Social Media

  • Write and design Instagram stories, providing art direction for these visual formats in collaboration with our inhouse creative team
  • Prepare content briefs and coordinate the design process with the creative team
  • Responsible for planning and scheduling daily social media posts across diverse platforms, optimizing the content calendar, and paid investment support in collaboration with the Social Media and Content Manager
  • Coordinate social media assets for retailers

Content

  • Manage relationships with agencies and copywriters
  • Develop new content projects and identify opportunities to leverage existing content in innovative ways
  • Assist with the coordination of special IWC shoots and provide administrative support for photo and video shoots
  • Collaborate with the Events, FOBs, PR, and Creative Center departments on content creation and project development
  • Responsible for developing and collaborating with the Team Lead, Social Media & Content, on our major partnerships and racing topics

Marketing campaigns

  • Support the coordination and implementation of the social media campaigns
  • Track campaign status, timelines, and implementation progress
  • Assist in campaign orchestration
  • Contribute to the development of post-campaign reports and performance summaries
