Full description

For acute care, rehabilitation and transitional care at the Cantonal Hospital we are looking for interns for assignments between 3 and 12 months.

Tasks

  • Assistance to smooth station operations through material maintenance, cleaning of equipment and carrying out messenger operations
  • Share responsibility for cleanliness and order as well as a patient-friendly service
  • Support of the nursing staff in their implementation in the care process (mobilization, nutrition, personal care)

Requirements

  • Minimum age 18 years
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Motivation and reliability
  • The joy of dealing with people
  • A quick understanding and pleasant manners
  • Collaboration in shift operations

We offer

Do you want to reconcile family and work well? With our in-house crèche directly on the site of the Cantonal Hospital, we support you for a healthy work-life balance that not only works, but also does well.

The hospitals in Schaffhausen include the Canton Hospital, the psychiatric services, the radiology and cardiology practices Posthof Neuhausen and the practice Neurology Schaffhausen Center. With more than 1,700 employees, we ensure the extended basic medical and nursing care in the Schaffhausen region. Every year, we look after approximately 11,000 inpatient and 90,000 outpatient patients. We are also an important training centre in the canton.
