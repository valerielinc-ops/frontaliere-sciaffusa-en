Shape your future – together with us!

To strengthen our successful team for the Schaffhausen area, we are looking for a customer-oriented personality with strong negotiation skills and a lot of commitment. For career changers and professionals with a local connection, we offer the perfect entry point with first-class training and development opportunities.

Whether you’re an insurance professional or a career changer – start your career in an agency at AXA, the No. 1! We support our customers as a competent partner – in every situation. You too?

This is what awaits you

  • Passionate commitment to our AXA customers
  • Needs-oriented advice for private customers in the areas of pensions and assets as well as health care
  • Continuous maintenance and expansion of existing customer relationships
  • Mediation of other AXA products (non-life) or services in the area of ​​advance care mandates and succession arrangements to external AXA partners
  • Acquiring new customers and building external networks

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed vocational training and further training in sales, trade or business administration
  • Passing the broker test (VBV) is very advantageous
  • High affinity for financial and pension products
  • Anchoring in the region
  • Convincing demeanor and good articulation skills
  • Winning and resilient personality combined with a high customer and service orientation
  • High independence and flexibility
  • Stylish knowledge of spoken and written German, other languages ​​are an advantage
  • Enjoyment of modern and contemporary digital tools

This is what we offer you

We create framework conditions that give you space to develop and fully exploit your potential. As a leading Swiss insurance company, we offer ambitious people attractive career opportunities and entrepreneurial freedom.

  • Discount of up to 30% on AXA products
  • 5 weeks vacation
  • Fleet discount/reimbursement of half-fare travelcard/GA (pro-rata)
  • Access to the Swibeco platform
  • Scope for independent action
  • Innovative structures, processes and modern tools
  • Sound, professional training and further education opportunities
